Sukoshi

Little by little, every week.

Sukoshi is a calm health journal for Android, for medicines you take weekly or daily. Record your doses, your weight and how you feel, look back over your history, and make a PDF to take to your appointments.

  • No account. You never sign up for anything.
  • Your journal stays on your phone. We can't see it.
  • Your history and exports are free, always.

Coming soon to Google Play.

Sukoshi is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. For advice about your health or treatment, talk to your prescriber, pharmacist or another healthcare professional.
