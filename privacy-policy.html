Sukoshi privacy policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026

1. Who we are

Sukoshi is a journal app for Android. It is made by Dare 2 Thread Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 12931577), registered office 25 Beechcroft Avenue, Birmingham, England, B28 9ER, United Kingdom. In this policy, "we" and "us" means Dare 2 Thread Ltd.

Questions about privacy: sukoshijournal@gmail.com

2. The short version

  • You don't need an account. You never sign up for anything.
  • Everything you record in Sukoshi stays on your phone. We can't see it.
  • Sukoshi has no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking.
  • If you buy Sukoshi Plus, Google Play handles the payment. We get a basic record of the sale, not your card details.

3. What Sukoshi stores on your phone

Sukoshi saves what you enter, in the app's private storage on your phone:

  • your medicine schedule, doses and the sites you note
  • weight, and waist and height if you choose to add them
  • symptoms, mood and notes
  • reminder times and app settings
  • whether you have bought Sukoshi Plus

Sukoshi also works things out from your entries on your phone, such as charts, percentage of starting weight lost and BMI. None of this is sent to us or anyone else.

Your data is protected by Android's built-in storage encryption. You can also turn on the app lock in Sukoshi. Sukoshi does not use Android's automatic cloud backup, so your journal is not copied to your Google account. Your own backup file is covered in section 4.

We can't see your data. It never reaches us, so we can't read it, share it, sell it or recover it for you.

To delete your data, use Your data > Delete everything in the app, or uninstall Sukoshi. Uninstalling removes the app's data from your phone. It does not remove any export or backup files you have saved elsewhere.

4. Exports and backups you make

You can make a PDF, CSV or JSON export, or an encrypted backup file. You choose where each file goes: your phone's storage, your own cloud storage, email, or another app.

  • We never receive these files.
  • Once you send a file somewhere, that service's own privacy terms apply.
  • Backup files are encrypted with a passphrase you choose. We never see the passphrase. If you lose it, nobody can open the backup, including us.
  • PDF and CSV exports are not encrypted. Keep them somewhere safe.

5. Reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your phone and shown by Android. They are not sent through our servers or any push service.

6. Buying Sukoshi Plus

Sukoshi Plus is an optional one-off purchase made through Google Play. Google handles the payment under its own terms and the Google Privacy Policy. We never see your card or bank details.

Google gives us a record of each sale. This includes the order number, the product, the price, the date, and your country, and your region or postcode where Google provides it. We use this to keep our accounts, meet tax rules and handle refunds. Our legal reason is that the law requires us to keep accounting records. We keep these records for 6 years after the end of the financial year they relate to.

Sukoshi checks on your phone whether you have bought Plus. It does not send your journal to Google or to us.

7. Health Connect (only if you turn it on)

If you choose to connect Health Connect, Sukoshi asks Android for permission to read your weight entries. Sukoshi stores the imported weights only on your phone, like everything else. It does not write anything to Health Connect. You can remove the permission at any time in Android's Health Connect settings.

The use of information received from Health Connect will adhere to the Health Connect Permissions Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write. We use it only to reply and to fix problems. We keep support emails for 12 months after the conversation ends, then delete them. Our legal reason is our legitimate interest in answering you.

Please don't send us your health details or screenshots of your journal. We don't need them to help you. If you do send them, we'll use them only to answer your question and delete them with the rest of the conversation.

9. What we don't do

  • No account, login or sign-up
  • No advertising and no advertising ID
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage tracking
  • No location, contacts, photos or microphone access
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone
  • No AI or automated decisions about you

10. Children

Sukoshi is for adults aged 18 and over. It is not designed for or aimed at children. Because we hold no journal data, there is nothing on our side to delete if a child has used it. A parent can delete the data by uninstalling the app.

11. Your rights

Your journal is on your phone, so you can view, export, correct or delete it yourself at any time.

For the information we do hold (sales records and emails), UK data protection law gives you rights to see it, correct it, ask us to delete it, object to how we use it, and restrict how we use it. Email sukoshijournal@gmail.com and we'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. We'd appreciate the chance to sort it out first.

12. Security

The journal is kept in the app's private storage, protected by Android's encryption and your optional app lock. Backup files use strong encryption with your passphrase. Sukoshi does not send journal data over the internet, so there is nothing for us to protect in transit.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects what happens to your data, we'll also tell you in the app before it takes effect.
